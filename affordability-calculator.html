How to Use This Affordability Calculator

Enter your annual income, monthly debt payments, down payment, and expected interest rate. The calculator uses standard front-end (28%) and back-end (36%) debt-to-income ratios to determine your maximum home price.

Understanding the Ratios

  • Front-End Ratio (28%) — your total housing payment should not exceed 28% of gross monthly income
  • Back-End Ratio (36%) — your total debt payments including housing should not exceed 36% of gross monthly income

These are general guidelines. Actual approval depends on your credit score, lender requirements, and local market conditions.
