How to Use This Debt Payoff Calculator

Enter your current credit card or loan balance, interest rate, minimum payment, and any extra amount you can pay each month. The calculator shows your payoff timeline and total interest.

Strategies to Pay Off Debt Faster

  • Debt avalanche — pay extra on the highest-interest debt first
  • Debt snowball — pay extra on the smallest balance first for motivation
  • Make biweekly payments — 26 half-payments equals 13 full payments per year

Even a small additional monthly payment can save you months of payments and hundreds or thousands in interest.
