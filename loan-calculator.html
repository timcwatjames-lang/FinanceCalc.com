How to Use This Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term in years. The calculator will show your estimated monthly payment, total interest paid, and total cost of the loan.

What Affects Your Loan Payment?

  • Loan amount — the more you borrow, the higher your payment
  • Interest rate — a lower rate saves you money over time
  • Loan term — longer terms mean lower monthly payments but more total interest

Use this tool to compare different scenarios before applying for a personal loan, auto loan, or any other installment loan.
