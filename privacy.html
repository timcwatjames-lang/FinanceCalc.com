Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Introduction

FinanceCalc ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website. It also covers your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information. Our calculators run entirely in your browser — no data you enter is sent to our servers.

  • Cookies & Tracking — We use cookies via Google AdSense to serve personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Usage Data — Google Analytics may collect anonymized data about page visits, device type, and browser (if you consent).
  • No Account Data — We do not require registration, and we do not collect names, emails, or payment information.

How We Use Your Data

  • To display relevant advertisements (via Google AdSense)
  • To analyze site traffic and improve our tools
  • To comply with legal obligations

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our site and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Google's use of advertising cookies is described in Google's Advertising Principles.

Your Rights Under GDPR (EU Visitors)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Change your cookie preferences at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us.

Your Rights Under CCPA (California Visitors)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Know — Request details about the data we collect and how it is used
  • Right to Delete — Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt Out — Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell data, but you can opt out of personalized ads)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us or adjust your cookie preferences using the consent banner.
