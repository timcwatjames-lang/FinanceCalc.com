How to Use This Compound Interest Calculator

Enter your initial investment, any periodic contributions, the expected annual return, and the time horizon. The calculator shows the future value and total interest earned.

The Power of Compounding

Compound interest is interest earned on interest. Over long time periods, it creates exponential growth. A $10,000 investment at 7% annual return grows to over $38,000 in 20 years without any additional contributions.

  • Start early — time is your biggest advantage
  • Contribute regularly — consistent additions add up dramatically
  • Higher frequency — more frequent compounding accelerates growth
