How to Use This Savings Calculator

Enter your starting balance, monthly deposit amount, expected annual interest rate, and savings period. The calculator projects your total balance and interest earned.

Tips for Growing Your Savings

  • Set up automatic transfers — consistency is key to building savings
  • High-yield savings accounts — earn 4-5% APY vs traditional bank rates
  • Increase deposits over time — raise your monthly deposit when you get a raise

Use this calculator to set savings goals for emergencies, vacations, or major purchases.
